About This Platform

India's Most Comprehensive
NGO Intelligence Platform

We built this directory to bring transparency, discoverability, and trust to India's vast civil society ecosystem — so the right people can find the right NGOs, and change can happen at scale.

The Problem We Solve

India Has 3.3 Million NGOs. Most Are Invisible.

Despite a massive civil society sector, discovery, verification, and accountability remain major challenges for all stakeholders.

❌ Before This Directory
  • 😕Donors had no single place to research and compare NGOs — information scattered across MCA, FCRA portal, NGO Darpan, individual websites.
  • 😕CSR teams spent weeks on manual due diligence before choosing NGO partners, with no standardised data format.
  • 😕Small NGOs in rural areas were invisible to national funders despite doing excellent work.
  • 😕Government bodies had no aggregated view of NGO presence and capacity in a given district or state.
  • 😕International funders couldn't easily verify Indian NGOs' FCRA status or financial health before partnerships.
  • 😕Researchers lacked structured, machine-readable data on India's civil society sector for analysis and policy work.
  • 😕Volunteers couldn't find relevant NGOs near them or understand what skills were actually needed.
✅ With This Directory
  • ✨One platform with all NGO information — registration, financials, leadership, impact, contact — verified and standardised.
  • ✨CSR teams filter by SDG, state, 80G status, and cause area in minutes. Complete due diligence information upfront.
  • ✨Every NGO — from a tiny village trust to a national organization — gets equal discoverability through search and filters.
  • ✨State-wise, district-wise NGO mapping enables targeted government programme implementation and monitoring.
  • ✨FCRA registration, PAN, registration type and number — all in one profile. Instant credibility checks.
  • ✨Structured, filterable data supports sector research, SDG impact analysis, and policy recommendations.
  • ✨Location-based search and skills matching helps volunteers connect with the right local NGOs.

🗂️ Our Data Schema — Key Features

Multi-source Verification

Registration details are cross-verified across MCA21, FCRA Portal, and Income Tax records to ensure accuracy before listing.

Standardised Format

All NGO data follows a uniform JSON schema enabling consistent comparison, filtering, and analysis across all 3.3 million organisations.

Real-time Updates

FCRA status, 80G approvals, and registration validity are updated quarterly from government portal feeds.

SDG & Impact Tagging

Every NGO is tagged with relevant UN SDGs based on their stated mission and programme areas, enabling impact-focused discovery.

Geographic Intelligence

NGOs are mapped at state, district, and pincode level. Both headquarters location and operational geographies are captured separately.

Accountability Rating

An A+ to C accountability rating is assigned based on FCRA filing compliance, annual report publication, audit completion, and board transparency.

About the Project

Why We Built This

The Vision

India has one of the largest civil society sectors in the world — over 3.3 million registered organisations working on everything from primary education in remote tribal areas to ocean conservation, from mental health helplines to disaster response.

Yet the sector suffers from a fundamental infrastructure gap: there is no single, comprehensive, verified, and regularly updated directory of Indian NGOs that serves all stakeholders — donors, CSR teams, government, funders, volunteers, and researchers alike.

This platform is our attempt to build that infrastructure — open, comprehensive, and built around the needs of every stakeholder in the development ecosystem.

🎯 Our goal: Every legitimate Indian NGO discoverable in under 60 seconds by any stakeholder, anywhere in the world.
