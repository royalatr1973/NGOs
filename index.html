About This Directory

The NGO Directory India is a comprehensive platform listing non-governmental organisations working across India. Each listing captures detailed information including registration details, FCRA numbers, PAN, annual budgets, team size, operational areas, awards, contact information, and more.

Our goal is to bring transparency and accessibility to India's vibrant social sector, connecting organisations with donors, volunteers, researchers, and policymakers.

Who Are the Users of This Website?

Individual Donors

People who want to donate to verified, transparent NGOs. They can research organisations, compare their impact, check registration details, and make informed giving decisions.

Corporate CSR Teams

Companies looking for NGO partners to fulfil their Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) mandates under Section 135 of the Companies Act. They can filter by sector and geography to find the right partners.

Volunteers

Individuals and groups seeking meaningful volunteer opportunities. They can discover NGOs in their city or state, learn about different causes, and connect directly with organisations.

Researchers & Academics

Students, professors, and think tanks studying India's social sector. They access data on sector distribution, geographic coverage, founding trends, and organisational scale.

Government & Policymakers

Government officials looking to partner with or understand the NGO landscape. They can identify organisations working on specific issues or in specific regions.

International Foundations & Donors

Global philanthropies and bilateral agencies seeking credible Indian partner organisations for grants and programme implementation.

Media & Journalists

Reporters and media professionals researching stories about social impact, NGO accountability, or specific social issues who need verified information about organisations.

Other NGOs & Peer Organisations

NGOs looking for collaboration, knowledge sharing, or understanding who else works in their sector or geography to avoid duplication and find partners.

What Value Does This Website Create?

Transparency & Trust

By listing registration numbers, FCRA details, PAN, tax exemption status, and financial data, the platform builds trust and enables verification of genuine organisations.

Efficient Resource Allocation

Donors and CSR teams can make data-driven decisions about where to direct funding, reducing wastage and ensuring funds reach credible organisations.

Strengthening the Social Sector Ecosystem

When NGOs are visible and discoverable, they attract more support. This directory acts as a multiplier for the entire social sector ecosystem in India.

Closing the Information Gap

India has over 3 million registered NGOs but information about them is scattered across government databases. This platform consolidates key data into one accessible place.

Enabling Collaboration

NGOs working in similar sectors or regions can discover each other, leading to partnerships, knowledge sharing, and reduced duplication of efforts.

Accountability & Governance

Public availability of organisation data incentivises better governance, financial discipline, and impact reporting among NGOs.

Volunteer Mobilisation

By making it easy to discover NGOs by location and cause, the platform helps match willing volunteers with organisations that need them, boosting civic participation.

Research & Policy Insights

Aggregate data on the NGO landscape (sector distribution, geographic gaps, founding trends) informs academic research and government policy design.
